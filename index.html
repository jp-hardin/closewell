    Questions

    What Closewell does, and what it doesn't.

    What is Closewell?

    Software for the sell side of a company sale. It holds the valuation, materials, buyer list and deal room in one record. Owners use it to run their own sale. Attorneys and boutique banks use Pro to run sales for clients.

    How is the valuation calculated?

    It starts from an EBITDA multiple band for your industry. It adjusts the multiple for size, growth, recurring revenue, customer concentration and how much the business depends on you, then applies it to adjusted EBITDA (reported EBITDA plus add-backs). The multiple bridge shows each adjustment.

    Who sees my business?

    Only the buyers you approve. Buyers see an anonymous teaser first. The full memo is released to a buyer only after their NDA is marked signed.

    Do you give legal, tax or investment advice?

    No. Closewell is software. Work with qualified legal, tax and financial advisors on your sale.

    Is the buyer list real?

    On this page the buyers are fictional samples that show how matching works. In the product, buyer research is built around your industry, size and region.
