    Questions

    What Closewell does, and what it does not.

    What is Closewell?

    Software for the sell side of a company sale. It holds the valuation, materials, buyer universe and deal room in one record. Owners use it to run their own sale. Counsel and boutique banks use Closewell Pro to run sales for clients.

    How is the valuation derived?

    It starts from an EBITDA multiple band for your industry. The multiple is adjusted for scale, growth, recurring revenue, customer concentration, owner dependence and margin, then applied to adjusted EBITDA (reported EBITDA plus add-backs). The multiple bridge shows each adjustment.

    Who sees my business?

    Only the counterparties you approve. Buyers see an anonymous teaser first. The full memorandum is released to a buyer only after their NDA is recorded as signed.

    Does Closewell provide legal, tax or investment advice?

    No. Closewell is software. Engage qualified legal, tax and financial advisors for your transaction.

    Are the buyers and transactions real?

    No. On this page, every buyer and transaction is a fictional example that shows how the platform works. In the product, buyer research is built around your industry, scale and region.
